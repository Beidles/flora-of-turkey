Words explained

Dicotyledon: traditional group of flowering plants with two seed leaves; modern botanical groupings can differ. Symmetry: how many ways a flower divides into matching halves. Petals: colored flower parts. Inflorescence: arrangement of flowers. Umbel: stalks spreading from one place like an umbrella. Head: many tiny flowers packed together.
