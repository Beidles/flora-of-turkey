Quick help for difficult words

Stamen = the part making pollen. Calyx = the green cup around the flower. Corolla = the colored petals. Bract = a small leaf beside a flower. Nutlets = small seed-like fruits. Hairy = look closely with a 10× hand lens. Whorl = flowers grouped around the stem. Upper and lower lip = the two main parts of many mint-family flowers.

Tip: If you cannot see a feature, do not guess. Inspect another flower or come back when fruit is present. Most questions need a flowering specimen. Written for a beginner around age 16.

    Explanation of difficult words

    Calyx: outer flower cup. Corolla: the petals. Bract: small leaf near a flower. Stamen: pollen-bearing part. Nutlet: small dry fruit. Axil: angle where a leaf meets a stem. Whorl: ring of flowers around the stem. Runner: spreading stem that roots. Awn: long pointed tip. Gland dots: tiny oil dots.

    Adapted from P. H. Davis, ed., Flora of Turkey and the East Aegean Islands, Volume 7, Labiatae key to genera, pages 37–42. This key identifies genera in that historical treatment, not species. Confirm with the descriptions in the Flora.
