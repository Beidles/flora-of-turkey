How the key works and its limits

Questions are selected to divide the remaining candidate genera as evenly as possible, while taking uncertainty into account. Unknown answers are skipped without excluding taxa. A genus receives a score when its recorded traits match; an uncertain or unspecified trait is not counted against it. The display is limited to five leading genera, but does not hide whether there are many plausible alternatives.

This prototype has only a curated sample of common or distinctive Lamiaceae genera, not the entire Flora of Turkey. Traits vary among species in a genus. Verify with the proper genus and species keys, especially calyx teeth, measurements, bracts and distribution.

Reference foundations: Davis, Flora of Turkey Vol. 7, Labiatae; revised Turkish Lamiaceae genus-key literature; Turkish Stachys diagnostic-character research. This example is intended for further development and should not be used for confirming medicinal or edible plant identity.
