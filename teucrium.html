This is an easier-to-read adaptation of the 1982 printed key (pages 53–56). Scientific names follow that edition and may have changed. Use the species descriptions to verify your result.

What do these words mean?

Calyx: small cup holding the bottom of a flower. Bract: small leaf beside a flower. Pedicel: stalk of one flower. Woody: hard like a twig. Axil: corner between leaf and stem. Runner: stem spreading along the ground. Woolly: covered in dense soft hairs.
