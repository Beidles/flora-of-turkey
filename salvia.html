Botanical word guide

Bract: small leaf beside flowers. Calyx: the cup around the flower base, remaining after petals fall. Corolla: petals together, including the tube and lips. Flower whorl: cluster around a stem. Glandular hair: a hair with a small gland at its tip. Filament: stalk supporting the pollen-producing anther. Connector: tissue joining the anther parts. Internal scales: small folds inside the flower tube. Terminal leaflet: the leaflet at the end of a divided leaf.
