    ▼ Before you start

    For Calamintha species treated in Flora of Turkey, Volume 7. This simplified species key has seven questions and eight possible results. Choose a flowering plant and use leaves from the middle of the stem.

    Use a hand lens for small hairs. Measure the colored flower (corolla), not the green cup (calyx). Count leaf teeth on just one side. Sizes overlap: check several leaves and more than one feature.

    Botanical word guide
    Corolla
    The colored flower; measure its full length.
    Calyx
    The green cup beneath the flower, ending in teeth.
    Lower calyx teeth
    The two teeth on the lower side of the calyx opening.
    Leaf teeth
    Points or rounded bumps along the leaf edge.
    Side veins
    Lines branching from the middle vein; look underneath the leaf.
    Flower cluster
    A group of flowers growing together.
