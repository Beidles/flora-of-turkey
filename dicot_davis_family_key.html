    Help with botanical words

    Stamen: pollen-making part. Carpel: seed-making part. Ovary: the part containing ovules, which develop into seeds. Superior ovary: other flower parts attach below it. Inferior ovary: flower parts attach above it. Perianth: petals and sepals. Glabrous: without hairs. Stipule: small leaf-like part near leaf base. Schizocarp: dry fruit that divides into parts. Locule: chamber inside an ovary.

    Reference: P. H. Davis (ed.), Flora of Turkey and the East Aegean Islands, Volume 1, Key to the Families, pp. 85–94. Adapted for educational use; no facsimile pages reproduced.
