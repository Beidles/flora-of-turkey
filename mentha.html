How to use

Use a flowering plant and a hand lens. Check several leaves from the main stem. Look closely at your plant. At each step, tap the description that fits best. Follow the choices until you reach a species. If a feature is unclear, use Go back and compare both routes. Sterility is not the same as having no ripe seeds today: it may need later observation.

Possible identification

Check all the features before deciding. This simplified historical key may not cover newly recognized species or name changes.

Source: P. H. Davis (ed.), Flora of Turkey and the East Aegean Islands, Volume 7, printed page 385; Mentha account by R. M. Harley. This follows the historical species key, including its hybrid names. Subspecies keys are not included.

Useful words: Calyx = green cup at the flower base; its tips are called teeth. Bracts = small leaves beside the flowers. Petiole = leaf stalk. Head = rounded flower cluster. Spike = elongated flower cluster. Sterile = unable to make viable seed. Felt-like = dense, matted hairs.

Mints often hybridize and can show mixed features. Check several characters before accepting a result. Works offline after saving this page.
