    Botanical word guide
    Corolla
    All the petals together.
    Calyx
    The cup and teeth around the flower base.
    Annulus
    A ring of hairs inside the flower tube.
    Bracteoles
    Small leaf-like parts close to the flowers.
    Amplexicaul
    A leaf base that clasps the stem.
    Glabrous
    Without hairs.
    Villous / woolly
    Covered in long soft hairs.
    Procumbent / decumbent
    Growing close to the ground.
    Verticillaster
    A group or ring of flowers around the stem.
