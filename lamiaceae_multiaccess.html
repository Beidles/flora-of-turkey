Botanical words explained

Stamen: pollen-producing part. Calyx: usually green cup around the flower. Corolla: the colored petals. Whorl: a ring of flowers around the stem. Bract: a small leaf beside a flower. Nutlet: small dry fruit. Glabrous: without hairs. Axil: where a leaf joins the stem. Style: the slender pollen-receiving structure. A 10× hand lens helps.

Why some genera remain

Each genus has a route through the original two-choice key. A choice only eliminates a genus when the original key explicitly gives it the opposite answer at that question. Genera for which that question does not apply remain possible. This conservative approach prevents overconfident identification, but some answers may not narrow the list much.
